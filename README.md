# 🥚 EFS Calculator — Egg Farm Simulator

A comprehensive web-based calculator for the Roblox game [Egg Farm Simulator](https://egg-farm-simulator-rblx.fandom.com/) by SirMing.

![EFS Calculator](https://img.shields.io/badge/EFS-Calculator-6c5ce7?style=for-the-badge)

## Features

### 📊 Dashboard
- Real-time summary of your farm stats
- DPS, Attack Damage, Effective DPS with crits
- Chickens per level, boss timer, eggception chance
- Smart recommendations based on your current progress

### 🧑‍🌾 Farmer Optimizer
- All 54 farmers with their costs, base DPS, and abilities
- DPS calculation with proper level scaling (4x per 25 levels above 200, 10x at 1000)
- DPS/WE ratio analysis to find the most efficient purchases
- Garbage Egg cost reduction factored in
- Optimal spending strategy suggestions

### 🥚 Easter Egg Planner
- All 22 Easter Eggs with accurate formulas and effects
- Three cost types: Exponential (2^n), Linear (n), Constant (1), and Turtle (n^1.5)
- Auto-distribution tool for the "Main 7" strategy
- Water Egg discount calculator
- Individual cost calculator for each egg

### 🔥 Phoenix Egg Planner
- All 8 Phoenix Eggs with GE costs
- Effect calculations with synergy multipliers
- Cumulative cost tracker

### ⬛ Black Egg Calculator
- Predict BE earnings at any farm level
- Accounts for Earth Egg, Radioactive Egg, and Rebirth Power
- BE earnings breakdown table by level checkpoints
- Golden Egg conversion calculator

### 🔄 Rebirth Planner
- Rebirth Power calculator from lifetime Golden Eggs
- Full retirement planner with BE → GE → RP projections
- Recommendation on whether it's worth retiring

### 🐔 Boss Fight Calculator
- Can you beat this boss? Enter your DPS and find out
- Factors in King Cobra, Acid, Hourglass, and Wind eggs
- DPS requirement table for common boss levels
- Boss HP and timer calculations

## Game Data
- All formulas sourced from the [Egg Farm Simulator Wiki](https://egg-farm-simulator-rblx.fandom.com/)
- Includes accurate farmer DPS scaling, Easter Egg formulas, Phoenix Egg effects
- Covers Black Egg earning formulas with Rebirth Power multipliers

## Usage
Simply open `index.html` in a browser. No server or dependencies required.
State can be saved/loaded from browser local storage.
